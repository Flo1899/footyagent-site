Not the manager. The agent.

Football agent game for iPhone & iPad

FootyAgent is a single-player football agent simulation for iPhone and iPad. You don't coach a club – you represent players. Scout talents, sign them as clients, negotiate their wages, playing time and transfers, and grow a one-desk office into a worldwide agency while rival agents compete for the same players. Free to download, playable offline, no ads, no account.

The negotiation

Every deal has a breaking point

Clubs have budgets, players have expectations – and neither tells you where the line is. Push too far and the club walks away, taking the transfer with it.

  • Three levers: playing-time role, contract length and wage.
  • Your cut: every client signs an agent contract with your commission on transfers and salary.
  • Reputation counts: deliver, and the better calls come to you.

How negotiations work →

Your clients

Turn rough diamonds into world stars

Every player has strength, potential, form and a character that reacts to your decisions. Potential is only an estimate until you sign him – the better your scouting, the sharper it gets.

  • Full history: transfers, ratings, titles and caps.
  • Keep the bond: neglected clients switch agencies.
  • Loans & moves: sometimes the smaller club is the smarter move.

How scouting works →

The climb

First the agent. Then the president.

Grow from a local agency to a Legendary Agency. Put your commissions into club shares – with more than half of the shares the club is yours: you set the strategy, upgrade stadium and academy and collect dividends.

  • 6 agency tiers: from local agency to Legendary Agency.
  • 4 licences: from national to legend – each one opens new doors.
  • 4 club strategies: from title chase to austerity, with real consequences.

Collectible

24 crests for your agency

6 are yours from the start. The rest are tied to your achievements – from your first transfer in bronze to the platinum order for Agent of the Year. Your crest sits next to your name in the rankings.
