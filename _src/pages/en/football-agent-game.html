Football Agent Game – Become the Agent, Not the Manager

FootyAgent is a football agent game for iPhone and iPad. Instead of coaching a club, you represent players: you scout them, sign them, negotiate their contracts and transfers, and earn a commission on every deal. It's single-player, free to download, playable offline and has no ads.

{{cta:feature_page}}

What is FootyAgent?

FootyAgent is a management simulation about the business side of football. You run a player agency: you find talented players, sign them as clients and steer their careers. Your income is commission – a share of each transfer fee and of your clients' wages, as agreed in every client's agent contract.

The game world spans {{n.nations}} nations with {{n.leagues}} leagues and {{n.clubs}} clubs. All players are fictional, and clubs are named after their cities. Seasons are simulated week by week, with league matches, domestic and continental cups and international call-ups.

How is it different from a football manager game?

In a football manager game you are responsible for one club. In FootyAgent you work for players spread across many clubs and leagues – and you never pick a line-up.

Scout players

New talents keep appearing in your scouting pool. You see their current strength, but for players you don't represent yet, potential is only an estimate shown as a range. Better scouting, a chief scout and scout contacts in a player's country make that range narrower. Scouting in detail

Represent players

Every client signs an agent contract with you: your share of his transfers, your share of his salary and how many seasons the contract runs. After that, his career is your job. Clients who sit on the bench, feel neglected or get a tempting call from a rival agent can leave you. Your office decides how many clients you can handle at once – from {{n.minClients}} at the start up to {{n.maxClients}}.

Negotiate contracts

When a client moves, you negotiate three things with the new club: his playing-time role, the contract length and his wage. The wage talks run over several rounds – the club can accept, counter or walk away, and the bigger your demand, the bigger the risk. Negotiations in detail

Transfers and loans

During the summer and winter transfer windows, clubs send offers for your clients to your inbox. You accept, decline or negotiate – or pitch a client to a club yourself. Loans to smaller clubs help young players get minutes. Transfers in detail

Build your agency

You start as a local agency and can rise through {{n.agencyTiers}} tiers up to a Legendary Agency. Expand your office, hire staff such as a chief scout, an analyst or a contract lawyer, earn {{n.licences}} licences and choose a specialisation. Become a brand with interviews, TV appearances and sponsors – and buy into clubs. Agency management in detail

Rivals and competition

Computer-controlled rival agents compete for the same players and try to poach your clients. Once you hold the continental licence, you can approach theirs. Season tasks, rival duels, the "Agent of the Year" award, {{n.achievements}} achievements and a ranking of {{n.agencies}} agencies keep score.
