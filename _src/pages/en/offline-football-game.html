Football game without internet

An offline football game for iPhone and iPad

Yes – FootyAgent is fully playable offline. The whole football agent career runs on your device without an internet connection, and you don't need an account. Online features are optional extras, not requirements.

{{cta:feature_page}}

What works offline

Everything that makes up the game:

  • scouting, signing and managing your clients,
  • all negotiations, transfers and loans,
  • simulated matches, leagues, cups and whole seasons,
  • your agency: office, staff, licences, sponsors and club shares,
  • achievements, crests, season tasks and the rankings of {{n.agencies}} agencies,
  • saving and loading your careers.

What uses the internet – and is optional

When your device is online, FootyAgent can also:

  • back up your saves to your iCloud (if iCloud is turned on),
  • process the optional {{app.proPack.name.en}} purchase or restore it,
  • send feedback you write in the app,
  • check whether an app update is available,
  • send gameplay statistics and end-of-season results – details are in the privacy policy.

None of this is needed to play. If you're offline, the game simply carries on.

Built for playing anywhere

FootyAgent is turn-based: nothing happens while the app is closed, and there are no energy bars or waiting timers. That makes it a good fit for flights, train rides or the underground – you advance a week whenever you like and pick up exactly where you left off.

What kind of game is it?

FootyAgent is a football management game with a twist: you're not the club manager but the players' agent. You scout talents, negotiate contracts and transfers and grow your agency. More about the football agent game
