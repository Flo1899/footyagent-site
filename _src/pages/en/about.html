About

About FootyAgent

FootyAgent is an independent football agent game for iPhone and iPad, made by {{app.developer}}, a solo developer from {{app.developerCountry.en}}. Design, code and support come from one person – every message to support lands with me personally.

The idea

Most football games put you on the touchline as a coach or in the boardroom as a club manager. FootyAgent looks at the game from a different seat: the agent who finds talent early, negotiates the contracts and makes the transfers happen. That's the whole game – the business behind the beautiful game.

What FootyAgent stands for

  • The full game for free. No paywall, no subscription. The optional {{app.proPack.name.en}} only adds save slots and crests.
  • No ads, no timers. Nothing pushes you to come back tomorrow or watch a video.
  • Offline and without an account. Your career lives on your device.
  • No pay-to-win. Money spent in the store never buys an advantage in the game.
