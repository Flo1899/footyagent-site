Short description

FootyAgent is a single-player football agent game for iPhone and iPad. Instead of coaching a club, you represent players: scout talents, negotiate their contracts and transfers, earn commission and grow a local agency into a worldwide one. Free, playable offline, no ads.

Long description

FootyAgent puts players in the chair that really pulls the strings in football – not the manager, not the player, but the agent. You discover raw talent across {{n.nations}} nations, sign players before rival agents do and steer their careers: agent contracts, wages, playing time, transfers and loans. Every deal has a hidden pain threshold; push too hard and the club walks away.

Along the way the agency grows from a one-desk office into a Legendary Agency: office upgrades, staff such as a chief scout, an analyst and a contract lawyer, {{n.licences}} licences, specialisations, sponsors and TV appearances. With enough money, agents can buy club shares and become a club's president. FootyAgent is a relaxed single-player management sim without timers, ads or an internet requirement, available in {{langcount}} languages.
