Price and in-app purchase

FootyAgent is free to download, and the complete game can be played for free – there are no ads, no energy bars and no subscriptions. There is {{app.proPack.what.en}}. With it you have {{n.saveSlotsPro}} save slots instead of {{n.saveSlotsFree}}, so you can run several careers side by side. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account and can be restored on your other devices.

Built for iPad, too

Since version 1.2.1, FootyAgent is a native iPad app: it uses the full screen with bigger text and more room for tables, player profiles and negotiations. Before that it was an iPhone-only app.

Offline play and iCloud

Your career runs entirely on your device, so you can play without an internet connection – on a plane, on the underground or anywhere else. If iCloud is turned on, FootyAgent also backs up your saves to your iCloud so you can get them back on a new device. More about playing offline

Is there an Android version?

Not at the moment. FootyAgent is currently available only for iPhone and iPad.
