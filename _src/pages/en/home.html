Not the manager. The agent.

Football agent game for iPhone & iPad

FootyAgent is a single-player football agent simulation for iPhone and iPad. You don't coach a club – you represent players. Scout talents, sign them as clients, negotiate their wages, playing time and transfers, and grow a one-desk office into a worldwide agency while rival agents compete for the same players. Free to download, playable offline, no ads, no account.
