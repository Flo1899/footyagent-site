Agency management in FootyAgent

In FootyAgent you build a football agency from a one-desk {{t.tiers.en.0}} into a {{t.tiers.en.5}}. You expand your office to take on more clients, hire staff, earn licences, choose specialisations, become a brand – and eventually buy into clubs.

Staff

{{n.staffRoles}} roles, each with a clear job:

  • {{t.staff.en.0}} – brings more and more talented players into your scouting pool.
  • {{t.staff.en.1}} – uncovers hidden information in negotiations, such as a club's pain threshold.
  • {{t.staff.en.2}} – raises your commission on transfers and salaries.
  • {{t.staff.en.3}} – looks after delegated clients on his own, for half the commission.
  • {{t.staff.en.4}} – gets injured clients back on the pitch sooner.
  • {{t.staff.en.5}} – handles media pressure and makes interviews more valuable.

Licences

There are {{n.licences}} licences, and each one opens new doors:

  • {{t.licences.en.0}} – where every agency starts.
  • {{t.licences.en.1}} – unlocks targeted poaching of rival agents' clients and your first specialisation.
  • {{t.licences.en.2}} – players rated 88 or higher only negotiate with agents at this level or above.
  • {{t.licences.en.3}} – a second specialisation; your name speaks for itself.

Specialisations

A specialisation gives your agency an identity – each with an advantage and a price:

  • {{t.specialisations.en.0}} – one extra talent in every batch and a sharper potential estimate; sponsors pay your clients 10 % less.
  • {{t.specialisations.en.1}} – better chances in every negotiation; clients are a little less satisfied.
  • {{t.specialisations.en.2}} – contacts become useful earlier and your rapport with clubs grows faster; you are worse at poaching.

Reputation and agency tiers

Your reputation (0–100) reflects the quality of your work and places your agency in one of {{n.agencyTiers}} tiers: {{t.tiers.en.0}}, {{t.tiers.en.1}}, {{t.tiers.en.2}}, {{t.tiers.en.3}}, {{t.tiers.en.4}} and {{t.tiers.en.5}}. A higher tier brings bigger sponsors and stronger talents who take your calls. You also compete in a ranking of {{n.agencies}} agencies.

Brand and sponsors

Successful agents become a brand: interviews, TV appearances and sponsor tiers bring extra income and help when you try to win over a rival's client.

Club shares and presidency

Commissions can be invested in club shares, which pay dividends. With more than half of a club's shares you become its president: you choose one of {{n.clubStrategies}} strategies – {{t.strategies.en.0}}, {{t.strategies.en.1}}, {{t.strategies.en.2}} or {{t.strategies.en.3}} – and upgrade the stadium and the academy. Promotion or relegation of your club has consequences for you, too.

Running costs and risk

Every upgrade and every employee raises your weekly costs. Expand too fast, or lose your best clients in a bad window, and debt piles up – a long stretch in the red puts your agency at risk.
